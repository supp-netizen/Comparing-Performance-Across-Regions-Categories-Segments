# 📊 Comparing Performance Across Regions, Categories & Segments

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Apex Retail's sales data** to understand how business performance varies across **regions, product categories, and customer segments**.

The goal of this analysis is to move beyond company-wide sales totals and provide a more detailed view of **which groups are performing strongly and which areas may require further attention**.

This project was completed as part of my ongoing **Data Analytics upskilling journey**, using hands-on analysis to answer realistic business questions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Regional operations leadership needs a clearer understanding of sales performance before the upcoming quarterly budget meeting.

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

- Which **regions** generate the highest sales?
- Which **product categories** contribute the most to overall performance?
- Which **customer segments** generate the most sales?
- Are there noticeable differences between groups?
- Which areas may require additional investigation or resources?
- How can data support better **budgeting and planning decisions**?

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 🌎 Regional Performance
Analyzed sales across different regions to identify:

- Highest- and lowest-performing regions
- Regional sales differences
- Contribution of each region to overall sales
- Potential areas for further investigation

### 📦 Product Category Performance
Compared sales across product categories to determine:

- Top-performing categories
- Lower-performing categories
- Category contribution to total sales
- Opportunities for additional analysis

### 👥 Customer Segment Performance
Analyzed sales by customer segment to understand:

- Which segments generate the most revenue
- Differences in purchasing behavior
- Segment-level performance
- Potential opportunities for targeted strategies

## 📊 Key Analysis Approach

The analysis followed a structured process:

**Raw Data → Data Preparation → Group-by Analysis → Comparison → Visualization → Business Insights**

Rather than looking only at overall sales, the dataset was broken down into meaningful business groups to identify differences in performance.

## 💡 Business Value

Segmenting sales data allows leadership to make more informed decisions instead of relying on a single company-wide metric.

The analysis can help support:

- 📈 Quarterly budgeting
